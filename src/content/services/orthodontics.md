## Orthodontic Solutions for All Ages

We offer comprehensive orthodontic care including:

### Traditional Braces
- Metal brackets and wires
- Most effective for complex cases
- Highly customizable
- Budget-friendly option

### Clear Aligners
- Nearly invisible treatment
- Removable for eating and cleaning
- Comfortable and convenient
- Ideal for mild to moderate cases

## Our Orthodontic Specialists

Our in-house orthodontists have years of experience:
- Treatment planning using 3D imaging
- Digital smile prediction
- Regular monitoring and adjustments
- Retention plans for long-term results

## Treatment Duration

Most cases take 18-24 months, depending on:
- Complexity of misalignment
- Age of patient
- Compliance with care instructions
- Treatment method chosen
