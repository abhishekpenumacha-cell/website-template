## What is Root Canal Treatment?

Root canal therapy is a procedure to treat infection or damage inside the tooth (the pulp). It's often the last option before extraction and can save your natural tooth.

## Why You Might Need It

- **Severe tooth decay**: When decay reaches the pulp
- **Repeated trauma**: From accidents or injuries
- **Deep cracks**: That expose the pulp chamber
- **Tooth pain**: Persistent pain while chewing or sensitivity

## Our Approach

We use advanced endodontic techniques to make the procedure:

- **Painless**: Complete anesthesia and comfort measures
- **Efficient**: Modern rotary instruments reduce treatment time
- **Precise**: Digital imaging and microscope-guided treatment
- **Effective**: High success rates with our specialist team

## After Treatment

- Temporary filling while tooth heals
- Permanent restoration (crown) recommended for longevity
- Follow-up visit to monitor healing
