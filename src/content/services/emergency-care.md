## We're Here for Emergencies

Dental emergencies don't wait for office hours. We provide same-day emergency appointments for:

- **Severe tooth pain**: With or without swelling
- **Broken or chipped teeth**: Traumatic injuries
- **Knocked-out teeth**: Time-sensitive treatment
- **Abscessed teeth**: Infected teeth with swelling
- **Lost fillings or crowns**: Temporary and permanent solutions
- **Severe bleeding**: After extraction or injury

## What to Do in a Dental Emergency

1. **Call us immediately**: We prioritize emergency cases
2. **First aid tips**:
   - For knocked-out teeth: Keep in milk or saliva
   - For broken teeth: Save the pieces if possible
   - For swelling: Apply cold compress
   - For pain: Over-the-counter pain relief
3. **Get seen quickly**: Same-day emergency slots available
