## Why Choose Dental Implants?

Dental implants are the gold standard for tooth replacement. They provide:

- **Natural appearance**: Indistinguishable from your natural teeth
- **Permanent solution**: Can last a lifetime with proper care
- **Improved functionality**: Chew and speak with confidence
- **Bone preservation**: Prevents jaw bone deterioration

## The Implant Process

Our multi-specialist team uses the latest technology including CBCT scanning and digital imaging to ensure precise placement. The process typically involves:

1. **Initial consultation** - Assessment and 3D imaging
2. **Implant placement** - Surgical insertion of titanium post
3. **Osseointegration** - 3-6 months for bone integration
4. **Crown placement** - Final restoration
